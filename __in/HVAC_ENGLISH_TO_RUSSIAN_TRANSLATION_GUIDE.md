## 2. Unit Conversions and Formulas

### 2.1 Temperature Conversions

**Celsius to Fahrenheit:**
$$T_F = \frac{9}{5}T_C + 32$$

**Fahrenheit to Celsius:**
$$T_C = \frac{5}{9}(T_F - 32)$$

**Examples:**
- 20°C (room temperature) = 68°F → translate as "20°C"
- 80°F (cooling design) = 26.7°C → round to "27°C" or "26-27°C"
- -10°C (winter) = 14°F → translate as "-10°C"
- Design temperature range: 21-24°C (instead of 70-75°F)

**Temperature Rise (ΔT):**
- 10°F = 5.56°C → round to "5-6°C" or "5.6°C"
- 15°F = 8.33°C → round to "8-9°C" or "8.3°C"

**Russian convention:** Use °C exclusively. In older Soviet texts, may see °K (Kelvin) for scientific calculations.

### 2.2 Power and Energy Conversions

| From | To | Formula | Multiply By |
|------|----|---------|----|
| BTU/h | kW | W ÷ 3,412.14 | 0.000293071 |
| kW | BTU/h | W × 3,412.14 | 3,412.14 |
| Ton of cooling | kW | Ton × 3.517 | 3.517 |
| kW | Ton of cooling | kW ÷ 3.517 | 0.284345 |
| W | BTU/h | W × 3.412 | 3.412 |
| kWh | BTU | kWh × 3,412.14 | 3,412.14 |

**Examples with translations:**
- 45 kW fan power = 153,348 BTU/h → translate as "45 кВт"
- 150 tons cooling = 527.6 kW → translate as "528 кВт" or "527.6 кВ"
- 5 kW heating = 17,061 BTU/h → translate as "5 кВ"

**Russian convention:** Use kW, kWh exclusively. Do NOT use "тонны холода" (tons of cooling) in modern technical documents—use kilowatts only.

### 2.3 Airflow Rate Conversions

| From | To | Formula | Multiply By |
|------|----|---------|----|
| CFM | m³/s | CFM ÷ 2,118.88 | 0.000471947 |
| m³/s | CFM | m³/s × 2,118.88 | 2,118.88 |
| CFM | m³/h | CFM × 1.699 | 1.699 |
| m³/h | CFM | m³/h ÷ 1.699 | 0.588 |
| m³/s | m³/h | m³/s × 3,600 | 3,600 |
| m³/h | m³/s | m³/h ÷ 3,600 | 0.000278 |

**Examples with translations:**
- 5,000 CFM = 2.36 m³/s → translate as "2.36 м³/с" or round to "2.4 м³/с"
- 10,000 CFM = 4.72 m³/s → translate as "4.7 м³/с"
- Typical office ventilation: 15 CFM/person = 0.007 m³/s per person → translate as "7 л/с на человека"

**Russian convention:** 
- Use m³/s for technical design documents
- Use m³/h for operating specifications
- Use л/с (liters per second) for small flows or ventilation per occupant
  - 1 m³/s = 1,000 L/s

| Pa | in. W.C. | Pa ÷ 249.082 | 0.00401865 |

### 2.6 Heat Capacity Conversions

| From | To | Formula | Multiply By |
|------|----|---------|----|
| BTU/(lb·°F) | kJ/(kg·K) | BTU/(lb·°F) × 4.1868 | 4.1868 |

- Air specific heat: 0.24 BTU/(lb·°F) = 1.005 kJ/(kg·K) → translate as "1.005 кДж/(кг·К)"

| From | To | Formula | Multiply By |
|------|----|---------|----|
| BTU/(h·ft²·°F) | W/(m²·K) | BTU/(h·ft²·°F) × 5.678 | 5.678 |
| W/(m²·K) | BTU/(h·ft²·°F) | W/(m²·K) ÷ 5.678 | 0.176110 |

**Examples with translations:**
- Window U-value: 0.30 BTU/(h·ft²·°F) = 1.70 W/(m²·K) → translate as "1.7 Вт/(м²·К)"
- Wall assembly: 0.06 BTU/(h·ft²·°F) = 0.34 W/(m²·K) → translate as "0.34 Вт/(м²·К)"

### 2.8 Area and Volume Conversions

| From | To | Formula | Multiply By |
|------|----|---------|----|
| ft² | m² | ft² × 0.092903 | 0.092903 |
| m² | ft² | m² × 10.7639 | 10.7639 |
| ft³ | m³ | ft³ × 0.0283168 | 0.0283168 |
| m³ | ft³ | m³ × 35.3147 | 35.3147 |
| 1,000 ft²/ton | m²/kW | Divide by 10.764, then multiply by 0.284345 | 0.0264 |

**Examples with translations:**
- 50,000 ft² office building = 4,645 m² → translate as "4,650 м²" or "4,645 м²"
- 10,000 ft³ room = 283 m³ → translate as "283 м³"
- 500 ft²/ton = 13.2 m²/кВ → translate as "13 м²/кВ"

### 4.5 Russian Mathematical/Scientific Conventions

**Decimal notation:**
- Use comma (,) for decimal separator in Russian text, not period (.)
- Example: 0,25 instead of 0.25
- Exception: In English-sourced equations or international standards, period (.) is acceptable in context

**Unit spacing:**
- Always include space before units: "25 °C" not "25°C"
- Exception: % is typically written without space: "60%" (though "60 %" is also correct)
- Compound units use spaces or multiplication sign: "W/(m²·K)" or "Вт/(м²·К)"

**Abbreviations in Russian:**
- Use small capitals or regular case followed by period
- Example: "м³/ч" (cubic meters per hour) not "куб.м/час"
- Always use metric abbreviations: м (meter), км (kilometer), м³ (cubic meter), etc.

**Equation formatting:**
- Use standard mathematical notation
- Explain variables in Russian
- Include units in brackets for clarity: P [кВ], h [ч], r [₽/кВ·ч]

## 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid When Translating HVAC Content

### 5.1 Unit Conversion Errors

**MISTAKE:** Converting only the numerical value without adjusting the formula or context
```
WRONG: "A 45 kW fan uses 45 BTU/h" 
       (This is completely false—45 kW = 153,348 BTU/h)
CORRECT: "A 45 kW fan uses 153,348 BTU/h, or approximately 153 k BTU/h"
         (In Russian context, just use 45 кВ without BTU conversion)
```

**MISTAKE:** Mixing imperial and metric in the same sentence
```
WRONG: "The 50,000 ft² building requires 2.36 м³/с of airflow"
       (ft² should be converted to m² first: 4,645 m²)
CORRECT: "The 4,645 m² building requires 2.36 m³/s of airflow"
```

**MISTAKE:** Failing to round appropriately for Russian industry
```
WRONG: "23.78°C design temperature"
       (Overly precise for design conditions)
CORRECT: "23-24°C design temperature" or "24°C"
         (Russian standards use integer or 0.5°C increments)
```

**MISTAKE:** Translating tons of cooling as "тонны"
```
WRONG: "150 тонн охлаждения" (150 tons of cooling)
       (Confusing with mass; Russian industry uses kW)
CORRECT: "528 кВ охлаждения" (528 kW of cooling capacity)
         or "холодопроизводительность 528 кВ"
```

### 5.2 Terminology Errors

**MISTAKE:** Translating technical acronyms rather than using Russian equivalents
```
WRONG: "Система переменного объема воздуха (Variable Air Volume)"
CORRECT: "Система с переменным расходом воздуха (VAV)" or just "VAV система"
```

**MISTAKE:** Using outdated Soviet-era terms
```
WRONG: "Батарея отопления" (heating battery—outdated term from Soviet era)
CORRECT: "Радиатор" (radiator) or "секционный радиатор" (sectional radiator)
         or "тепловой аппарат" (heating apparatus)
EXCEPTION: "Батарея" is still acceptable when referring to plate heat exchangers: 
           "Теплообменная батарея"
```

**MISTAKE:** Using brand names generically
```
WRONG: "использовать фреон" (use Freon)
       (Freon is DuPont trademark for specific refrigerants)
CORRECT: "использовать хладагент R-410A" (use refrigerant R-410A)
         or "использовать экологичный хладагент" (use environmentally friendly refrigerant)
```

**MISTAKE:** Confusing "вентиляция" with "вентилятор"
```
WRONG: "Система вентилятора обеспечивает свежий воздух"
       (This says "fan system" instead of "ventilation system")
CORRECT: "Система вентиляции обеспечивает свежий воздух"
         (Ventilation system provides fresh air)
```

### 5.3 System Design Context Errors

**MISTAKE:** Applying US design standards without adjusting for Russian climate
```
WRONG: Directly translating "70-75°F indoor design = 21-24°C" 
       without considering Russian winter expectations
CORRECT: For Russian context in St. Petersburg or Moscow:
         Winter design: 20-21°C (Russian preference for cooler interior)
         Summer design: 23-24°C (Russian preference for cooler interior)
         Adjust based on climate zone and local codes
```

**MISTAKE:** Using US ventilation rates without noting Russian requirements are higher
```
WRONG: "The office ventilation rate is 15 CFM per person"
       (Without noting Russian standard is 40-60 m³/h per person)
CORRECT: "The office ventilation rate is 7.1 L/s per person [US standard: 15 CFM/person]
          Note: Russian standards (СНиП 2.04.05-91) require 40-60 m³/h (11-17 L/s) per person"
```

**MISTAKE:** Ignoring Russian humidity comfort preferences
```
WRONG: "Design for 30-50% relative humidity" (US standard)
CORRECT: For Russian residential spaces: "Design for 30-60% relative humidity (ГОСТ 30494)"
         For Russian offices: "Design for 40-60% relative humidity"
```

### 5.4 Linguistic and Grammar Errors

**MISTAKE:** Direct word-for-word translation creating awkward phrasing
```
WRONG: "Тепловой обменник восстанавливает потерянное тепло"
       (Literally: "Heat exchanger restores lost heat")
CORRECT: "Теплообменник рекуперирует тепло из вытяжного воздуха"
         (Heat exchanger recovers heat from exhaust air)
```

**MISTAKE:** Using masculine form of adjective with feminine noun (grammatical error)
```
WRONG: "Холодный производство энергии"
CORRECT: "Холодная производительность" (feminine to match производительность)
         or "Холодопроизводительность" (compound form, preferred)
```

**MISTAKE:** Incorrect prepositional case in technical context
```
WRONG: "в теплообменнике воздуха" (in the air heat exchanger)
       (Should use genitive case after "в" + prepositional case)
CORRECT: "в теплообменнике воздуха" is actually correct, but better:
         "в воздушном теплообменнике" (in an air-type heat exchanger)
```

**MISTAKE:** Inconsistent terminology within the same document
```
WRONG: Using both "нагнетатель" and "вентилятор" interchangeably
       without clarifying the difference
CORRECT: Define once at beginning: "вентилятор (или нагнетатель)" 
         and use consistently throughout
```

### 5.5 Contextual and Cultural Errors

**MISTAKE:** Applying US energy costs without noting differences
```
WRONG: "Annual energy cost = $21,681" (direct translation without conversion)
CORRECT: Convert to Russian currency with context:
         "Annual energy cost ≈ 2.17 million rubles*" 
         (*at 2026 exchange rates, approximately [rate] ₽/USD)
         Or better: Use generic cost units or ratios to avoid confusion
```

**MISTAKE:** Referencing US regulations without Russian equivalents
```
WRONG: "Per ASHRAE 90.1, systems must meet these requirements..."
       (Without noting equivalent Russian standard)
CORRECT: "Per ASHRAE 90.1 (equivalent to СП 60.13330.2012 in Russia), 
          systems must meet these requirements..."
```

**MISTAKE:** Not accounting for different building practices in Russia
```
WRONG: Directly translating ventilation approaches designed for US buildings
       (e.g., US preference for return air through plenums vs. Russian preference 
        for ductwork)
CORRECT: Note differences in design philosophy and adapt terminology
         to Russian industry practice where applicable
```

## 6. Formatting Rules for Russian HVAC Translations

### 6.1 Front Matter (YAML) Structure

All Russian HVAC documents should follow this Hugo front matter structure:

```yaml
---
title: "Название статьи на русском языке"
description: "Краткое описание содержания на русском языке, содержащее основные ключевые концепции."
date: 2026-04-14
tags: ["тег-1", "тег-2", "тег-3", "термин-на-русском"]
categories: ["Категория ОВКВ", "Подкатегория"]
weight: 3
lang: "ru"
original_lang: "en"
---
```

**Front matter field requirements:**

| Field | Requirements | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| `title` | Full title in Russian; 50-80 characters | `"Экономический анализ систем ОВКВ"` |
| `description` | 150-200 character summary with key terms | `"Комплексная оценка экономической эффективности систем отопления, вентиляции и кондиционирования..."` |
| `date` | ISO 8601 format (YYYY-MM-DD) | `2026-04-14` |
| `tags` | 6-10 Russian keywords, lowercase, hyphenated | `["анализ-затрат", "окупаемость", "овкв"]` |
| `categories` | 2-3 logical groupings | `["Экономика ОВКВ", "Проектирование"]` |
| `weight` | Navigation priority (1-5) | `2` |
| `lang` | Language code | `"ru"` |
| `original_lang` | Source language | `"en"` |

### 6.2 Markdown Structure and Formatting

**Heading hierarchy:**
```markdown
# Основное название (H1 - page title in body)
## Основной раздел (H2 - main sections)
### Подраздел (H3 - subsections)
#### Дополнительный уровень (H4 - detailed topics)
```

**Spacing conventions:**
- One blank line between heading and text
- One blank line between paragraphs
- Two blank lines before new major section (H2)

**Lists in Russian:**
```markdown
## Компоненты системы ОВКВ

- Установка обработки воздуха (УОВ)
- Воздуховоды и арматура
- Регулирующие устройства:
  - Дроссельные заслонки
  - Регуляторы расхода
  - Обратные клапаны

1. Этап проектирования
2. Этап закупок
3. Этап монтажа
```

**Emphasis formatting:**
- **Bold** for key terms being defined: "**Холодопроизводительность** — это..."
- *Italic* for foreign terms or emphasis: "согласно *ASHRAE Standard 62.1*"
- `Code` for technical abbreviations and values: "`ГОСТ 30494`", "`2.36 м³/с`"

### 6.3 Equation and Formula Formatting

Use LaTeX for mathematical expressions:

```markdown
Сенсибельная тепловая нагрузка:

$$Q_s = \frac{H_s}{\rho c_p \Delta T}$$

Где:
- $Q_s$ — сенсибельная тепловая нагрузка вентиляции (м³/с)
- $H_s$ — полная сенсибельная теплопроизводительность (кВ)
- $\rho$ — плотность воздуха (кг/м³)
- $c_p$ — удельная теплоемкость воздуха (кДж/(кг·К))
- $\Delta T$ — допустимый перепад температуры (°C или К)
```

**Variable naming in Russian:**
- Use descriptive variable names where appropriate
- Provide both variable symbol and Russian definition
- Always include units in parentheses or brackets

### 6.4 Table Formatting for Russian Content

```markdown
| Параметр | Объем (м³/ч) | Давление (Па) | Примечание |
|----------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Приточный воздух | 2,400 | 180 | Стандартные условия |
| Вытяжной воздух | 2,100 | -220 | Включая утечки |
```

**Table conventions:**
- Use comma as decimal separator in Russian numerical content: `2,400` not `2400`
- Include units in header row
- Right-align numerical columns
- Left-align text columns
- Provide explanatory notes column when helpful
- For large tables, provide summary rows with totals

### 6.5 Cross-References and Links

**Internal links in Russian content:**

### 6.6 Special Formatting for Technical Content

**For numbered calculations:**
```markdown
### Расчет холодопроизводительности

**Дано:**
- Сенсибельная тепловая нагрузка: 50 кВ
- Скрытая тепловая нагрузка: 15 кВ
- Требуемая холодопроизводительность = ?

**Решение:**

Общая холодопроизводительность:
$$Q_{total} = Q_{sensible} + Q_{latent} = 50 + 15 = 65 \text{ кВ}$$

**Результат:** Требуемая холодопроизводительность составляет 65 кВ
```

**For definitions and terminology boxes:**
```markdown
> **Определение:** Холодопроизводительность — количество теплоты, удаляемой 
> холодильной машиной в единицу времени, выраженное в киловаттах (кВ).
```

**For warnings and important notes:**
```markdown
⚠️ **Внимание:** Все расчеты должны выполняться в соответствии с 
требованиями СНиП 2.04.05-91 и ГОСТ 30494.
```

### 6.7 Unit and Notation Standards in Russian Text

**Spacing around units:**
```
Correct: 25 °C (space before °C)
Correct: 2,400 м³/ч (space before unit)
Correct: 180 Па (space before unit)
Correct: 60% (no space before %)
```

**Decimal notation:**
```
In technical tables and calculations: 2,35 м³/с (comma as separator)
In informal text: 2.35 м³/с (period acceptable in this context)
Percentage: 60% or 60 % (both acceptable)
```

**Temperature notation:**
```
Single value: 20 °C
Range: 20-22 °C or 20 °C - 22 °C
Delta: ΔT = 5 °C
```

**Energy and power:**
```
Power: кВ (kilowatt), not k Вт or кВ (use abbreviation)
Energy: кВ·ч (kilowatt-hour), written with middle dot
Capacity: холодопроизводительность 528 кВ
```

### 6.8 Russian-Specific Markdown Conventions

**Abbreviations:**
- Write out first occurrence with abbreviation in parentheses
- Use abbreviation subsequently
- Example: "Система автоматизации здания (САЗ) управляет всеми компонентами. САЗ обеспечивает оптимальную работу..."

**Proper noun capitalization:**
- Keep English proper nouns capitalized: ASHRAE, LEED, AHRI
- Capitalize Russian proper nouns normally: Россия, Москва, ГОСТ
- Technical terms in Russian: lowercase unless starting sentence

**Date and time:**
- Use Russian date format: 14 апреля 2026 г. or 2026-04-14
- Time: 14:30 (24-hour format)
- Seasons: spring (весна), summer (лето), autumn (осень/падение), winter (зима)
