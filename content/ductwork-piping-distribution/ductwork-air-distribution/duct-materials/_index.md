## Overview

Duct material selection directly impacts system performance, longevity, and installation cost. The choice depends on application requirements including air velocity, temperature, humidity, chemical exposure, and installation constraints. SMACNA standards provide the technical foundation for proper material selection and construction methods.

## Material Properties and Applications

### Galvanized Steel

Galvanized steel remains the industry standard for commercial HVAC applications due to its optimal balance of strength, durability, and cost.

**Key Properties:**
- Gauge range: 30 gauge (0.0157") to 16 gauge (0.0635")
- Maximum operating temperature: 450°F continuous
- Thermal conductivity: 26.0 BTU/(hr·ft·°F)
- Corrosion resistance: zinc coating provides 7-15 years protection in normal environments

**Applications:**
- Commercial building supply and return air systems
- Medium to high-pressure applications (up to 10" w.g.)
- General ventilation and exhaust systems
- Clean air environments without significant corrosive contaminants

**SMACNA Requirements:** Sheet metal gauge selection follows SMACNA pressure class specifications. For rectangular ducts, minimum gauge increases with duct dimension and pressure class.

### Aluminum

Aluminum offers superior corrosion resistance in specialized environments where galvanized steel fails prematurely.

**Key Properties:**
- Density: 0.098 lb/in³ (33% lighter than steel)
- Thermal conductivity: 118 BTU/(hr·ft·°F) (4.5× higher than steel)
- Corrosion resistance: excellent in coastal and high-humidity environments
- Maximum operating temperature: 400°F

**Applications:**
- Coastal installations with salt air exposure
- Chemical processing facilities with acidic vapors
- Clean rooms requiring non-ferrous materials
- Systems requiring reduced structural load

**Considerations:** Higher thermal conductivity increases heat transfer, requiring additional insulation in conditioned spaces. Material cost typically 2.5-3× galvanized steel.

### Stainless Steel

Stainless steel provides maximum durability in the most demanding environments.

**Key Properties:**
- Common alloys: 304 and 316 stainless
- Maximum operating temperature: 1500°F (intermittent)
- Corrosion resistance: superior in chemical and high-moisture environments
- Smooth surface finish: Ra 32 microinch or better

**Applications:**
- Laboratory exhaust systems handling corrosive fumes
- Kitchen exhaust systems with grease-laden air
- Pharmaceutical manufacturing clean rooms
- High-temperature exhaust applications

**Cost Factor:** Material and fabrication costs reach 4-6× galvanized steel, justified only when other materials fail performance or longevity requirements.

### Fiberglass Duct Board

Rigid fiberglass duct board combines air barrier and thermal insulation in a single product.

**Key Properties:**
- Density: 3-6 lb/ft³
- Thermal resistance: R-4.2 to R-8.0 per inch thickness
- Maximum air velocity: 2500 fpm (SMACNA recommendation)
- Maximum operating temperature: 250°F
- Flame spread/smoke developed: 25/50 (meets UL 181 Class 1)

**Applications:**
- Low to medium-pressure systems (up to 2" w.g.)
- Supply and return ducts in conditioned spaces
- Situations requiring integral thermal and acoustic insulation
- Residential and light commercial installations

**Limitations:** Not suitable for high-velocity systems, outdoor exposure, or high-moisture environments. Requires careful handling to prevent damage to air barrier coating.

### Fabric Ductwork

Engineered fabric duct systems provide uniform air distribution through porous textile or engineered orifices.

**Key Properties:**
- Materials: polyester, nylon, or fiberglass fabrics
- Air permeability: engineered from 0-400 CFM/ft²
- Maximum static pressure: 4" w.g. (typical)
- Weight: 0.1-0.3 lb/ft² (95% lighter than sheet metal)

**Applications:**
- Industrial spaces requiring uniform air distribution
- Food processing facilities (washable, antimicrobial options)
- Temporary or seasonal installations
- High-bay warehouses and manufacturing facilities

**Advantages:** Eliminates hot/cold spots through 360° dispersion, simple suspension systems, and machine-washable maintenance.

## Construction Standards

**SMACNA Specifications:**
- HVAC Duct Construction Standards - Metal and Flexible
- Fibrous Glass Duct Construction Standards
- Round Industrial Duct Construction Standards

**Key Requirements:**
- Transverse joint spacing: maximum 48" for galvanized steel, 10' for fiberglass
- Longitudinal seams: Pittsburgh, snap-lock, or welded per pressure class
- Reinforcement: required when duct dimensions exceed SMACNA tables for given gauge and pressure
- Leakage class: Seal Class A, B, or C based on system requirements

## Installation Considerations

**Material Handling:**
- Galvanized steel: protect zinc coating from abrasion during handling
- Aluminum: avoid contact with dissimilar metals (galvanic corrosion)
- Fiberglass: maintain air barrier integrity, seal all joints per manufacturer specifications
- Fabric: ensure proper tension and support spacing per engineering specifications

**Joinery Methods:**
- Metal ducts: drive cleats, S-slips, flanged connections per SMACNA
- Fiberglass: stapled and taped joints, fiberglass-to-metal transition collars
- Fabric: suspension cables, support rings, and manufacturer connection systems

**Field Verification:**
Test installed systems for leakage per SMACNA Seal Class requirements. Maximum allowable leakage varies from 48 CFM/100 ft² (Seal Class A) to 12 CFM/100 ft² (Seal Class C) at test pressure.
