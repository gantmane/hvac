HVAC air filtration encompasses diverse filter types, each optimized for specific applications, efficiency requirements, and operating conditions. Understanding filter construction and performance characteristics enables appropriate selection for varied applications.

## Mechanical Filtration

### Panel Filters

Panel filters represent the simplest and most economical filtration option.

**Construction**:
- Flat fibrous media in rigid cardboard or metal frame
- Media: fiberglass, polyester, or synthetic fibers
- Thickness: 1-2 inches typical
- Disposable design

**Performance Characteristics**:
- MERV 1-4 ratings
- Initial pressure drop: 0.05-0.15 in. w.g.
- Efficiency: 3-10 μm particles primarily
- Dust holding: 100-300 g/m²

**Applications**:
- Residential furnaces and AC
- Prefilters for higher efficiency stages
- Equipment protection (minimal IAQ requirements)

### Pleated Filters

Pleated filters increase media area through accordion-style folds, dramatically improving dust holding capacity and efficiency.

**Construction**:
- Synthetic or blended media in pleated configuration
- Pleat counts: 1-2 pleats per inch typical
- Wire backing or self-supporting media
- Standard sizes: 1", 2", 4" depths

**Media Types**:

| Media | Characteristics | Efficiency |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| Polyester | Durable, moisture resistant | MERV 7-10 |
| Electret | Electrostatically charged | MERV 8-13 |
| Synthetic blend | Balanced performance | MERV 8-11 |
| Microfiber | High efficiency | MERV 13-16 |

**Performance**:
- MERV 5-16 range
- Initial pressure drop: 0.15-0.50 in. w.g.
- Extended surface: 5-15× face area
- Service life: 3-12 months

### Bag Filters (Pocket Filters)

Bag filters provide maximum media area for extended service intervals.

**Construction**:
- Multiple synthetic media pockets
- Header assembly with rigid frame
- Pocket depths: 12-36 inches
- 4-12 pockets per filter

**Advantages**:
- Very high dust holding capacity (2-5 kg)
- Extended service intervals (12-24 months)
- Low operating pressure drop per efficiency level

**Applications**:
- Commercial and industrial AHUs
- Final filters in multi-stage systems
- High airflow applications

### Cartridge Filters

Cylindrical cartridge filters suit specialized applications.

**Construction**:
- Pleated media in cylindrical form
- Metal end caps and center core
- Various media options

**Applications**:
- Dust collectors
- Compressed air filtration
- Process exhaust systems

## High-Efficiency Filters

### HEPA Filters

High-Efficiency Particulate Air filters represent the standard for critical particle removal.

**Construction**:
- Submicron glass fiber media
- Deep pleating (50-100 pleats per linear foot)
- Separators: aluminum or paper
- Metal frame with gasket seal

**Standards**:
- US: 99.97% @ 0.3 μm (IEST-RP-CC001)
- European H13: 99.95% @ MPPS
- European H14: 99.995% @ MPPS

**Applications**:
- Hospital operating rooms and isolation
- Pharmaceutical manufacturing
- Semiconductor cleanrooms
- Nuclear facilities

### ULPA Filters

Ultra-Low Penetration Air filters exceed HEPA performance.

**Specifications**:
- U15: 99.9995% @ MPPS
- U16: 99.99995% @ MPPS
- U17: 99.999995% @ MPPS

**Applications**:
- ISO Class 3-4 cleanrooms
- Critical pharmaceutical processes
- Microelectronics manufacturing

### Mini-Pleat HEPA

Modern mini-pleat designs reduce pressure drop while maintaining efficiency.

**Advantages**:
- 50% lower pressure drop vs. traditional HEPA
- No separators (eliminates aluminum waste)
- Reduced cabinet depth requirements
- Improved energy efficiency

## Electronic Air Cleaners

### Electrostatic Precipitators

ESPs use high-voltage ionization and collection plates.

**Operating Principle**:
1. Corona discharge ionizes particles (12-15 kV)
2. Charged particles migrate to grounded plates
3. Particles adhere to collection surface
4. Plates cleaned periodically (washable)

**Performance**:
- High initial efficiency (90-95%)
- Low pressure drop (0.05-0.15 in. w.g.)
- Ozone generation concern (≤0.05 ppm required)
- Efficiency decreases with plate loading

### Polarized Media

Combines mechanical filtration with electrostatic enhancement.

**Construction**:
- Disposable media panels
- Active electrostatic field across media
- Lower voltage than ESP (6-8 kV)

**Advantages**:
- Higher efficiency than passive media
- Lower maintenance than ESP plates
- Reduced ozone generation

### Needlepoint Ionization

Bipolar ionization systems charge particles for enhanced capture.

**Operating Principle**:
- Carbon fiber brushes emit positive and negative ions
- Ions attach to airborne particles
- Agglomerated particles captured by downstream filters
- Claims include pathogen inactivation

**Considerations**:
- Effectiveness varies by installation
- Verify ozone and byproduct generation
- Requires maintenance of emitter points

## Specialized Filter Types

### Photocatalytic Oxidation (PCO)

UV light activates titanium dioxide catalyst to oxidize organic contaminants.

**Applications**:
- VOC destruction
- Odor control
- Supplemental biological control

**Limitations**:
- Incomplete oxidation may produce aldehydes
- Catalyst deactivation over time
- Energy consumption for UV lamps

### Activated Carbon

Adsorption media for gaseous contaminant removal.

**Forms**:
- Granular beds
- Pleated panels with carbon loading
- Bonded carbon fiber media
- Impregnated carbon for specific gases

**Applications**:
- VOC removal
- Odor control
- Acid gas removal (with impregnation)

### Molecular Sieves

Synthetic zeolites with defined pore sizes.

**Advantages**:
- Selective adsorption by molecular size
- Regenerable
- High capacity for specific compounds

## Filter Comparison Summary

| Type | MERV Range | Typical ΔP | Dust Holding | Best Application |
|------|------------|------------|--------------|------------------|
| Panel | 1-4 | 0.05-0.15" | Low | Prefilter, residential |
| Pleated | 5-16 | 0.15-0.50" | Medium | Commercial general |
| Bag | 10-15 | 0.20-0.40" | High | Long service intervals |
| HEPA | 17-20 | 0.50-1.50" | Medium | Critical environments |
| ESP | 85-95%* | 0.05-0.15" | N/A (washable) | Low pressure drop needs |

Selection among these filter types requires balancing efficiency requirements, pressure drop constraints, maintenance considerations, and total cost of ownership for each specific application.
