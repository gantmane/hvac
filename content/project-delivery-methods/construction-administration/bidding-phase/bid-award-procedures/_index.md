Bid award procedures establish the formal transition from competitive bidding to contract execution for HVAC construction projects. These procedures ensure legal compliance, financial security, and proper project initiation.

## Award Notification

### Notification Timeline

Award notification follows established protocols:

**Public Projects:**
- Formal board or commission approval required
- Public announcement of award decision
- Notification within 30-60 days of bid opening
- Posting of results on public platforms
- Written notification to all bidders

**Private Projects:**
- Owner determines notification timeline
- Typically 14-30 days after bid opening
- Direct communication to selected contractor
- Optional notification to unsuccessful bidders

### Notification Content

Award letters contain specific information:

**Required Elements:**
- Project identification and location
- Contract amount and scope
- Required submittal deadlines
- Bond and insurance requirements
- Contract execution timeline
- Conditions of award

**HVAC-Specific Items:**
- Equipment submittal requirements
- Long-lead item procurement
- Coordination meeting schedules
- BIM/CAD file exchange protocols
- Factory witness test requirements

### Protest Period

Time allowed for bid challenges:

**Public Projects:**
- 5-10 business day protest period
- Formal protest procedures required
- Written documentation of concerns
- Suspension of award during review
- Resolution before contract execution

**Private Projects:**
- Owner discretion on protest handling
- Typically no formal protest rights
- Business relationship considerations

## Performance Bonds

### Bond Requirements

Financial guarantee of contract completion:

**Standard Coverage:**
- 100% of contract value
- Extends 1 year beyond completion
- Covers labor and material defaults
- Includes correction period obligations

**HVAC Considerations:**
- Equipment warranty periods
- System performance guarantees
- Commissioning completion
- Seasonal testing requirements
- Multi-year maintenance periods

### Surety Qualifications

Acceptable bonding companies:

**Rating Requirements:**
- A.M. Best rating of A- or better
- Treasury Department approved
- State-licensed surety company
- Single project limit verification
- Aggregate capacity confirmation

**For Large HVAC Projects:**
- Bonding capacity $10M minimum
- Mechanical trade experience
- Equipment manufacturer relationships
- Claims history review

### Bond Forms

Standardized documentation:

- AIA Document A312 (standard)
- Federal Form 25 (government projects)
- Custom forms (owner-specific)
- Dual obligee arrangements
- Consent of surety requirements

## Payment Bonds

### Coverage Scope

Protection for subcontractors and suppliers:

**Protected Parties:**
- First-tier subcontractors
- Second-tier subcontractors (limited)
- Material suppliers
- Equipment manufacturers
- Rental companies
- Labor providers

**HVAC-Specific Coverage:**
- Chiller manufacturers
- Controls suppliers
- Ductwork fabricators
- Piping contractors
- Testing agencies
- Commissioning providers

### Claim Procedures

Process for bond claims:

**Notice Requirements:**
- 90-day notice after last work
- Written claim to contractor and surety
- Documented work performed
- Proof of non-payment
- Contract relationship verification

**HVAC Material Claims:**
- Equipment delivery documentation
- Shop drawing approval evidence
- Installation verification
- Manufacturer warranties
- Final balancing reports

### Bond Amount

Coverage levels:

- 100% of contract value (typical)
- 50% minimum (some jurisdictions)
- State-specific requirements
- Federal Miller Act compliance
- Little Miller Acts (state projects)

## Insurance Requirements

### Commercial General Liability

Primary liability coverage:

**Minimum Limits:**
- $1,000,000 per occurrence
- $2,000,000 aggregate
- Products/completed operations
- Contractual liability
- XCU coverage (explosion/collapse/underground)

**HVAC Enhancements:**
- Refrigerant release coverage
- Pollution liability for oil/glycol
- Vibration/settling damage
- Water damage from testing
- Equipment damage during installation

### Workers Compensation

Statutory employee coverage:

- State-mandated limits
- Employer's liability $1M minimum
- All states endorsement
- Waiver of subrogation
- Certificate for each subcontractor

### Automobile Liability

Vehicle coverage requirements:

- $1,000,000 combined single limit
- Owned/hired/non-owned vehicles
- Out-of-state coverage
- Equipment transportation
- Delivery vehicle coverage

### Builders Risk Insurance

Property coverage during construction:

**Owner-Purchased (typical):**
- All-risk coverage
- 100% completed value
- Includes materials on-site
- Transit coverage
- Testing coverage

**HVAC Equipment Protection:**
- Factory-to-site transit
- Storage at staging areas
- Rigging and installation
- Hydrostatic testing damage
- Temporary heating/cooling equipment

### Professional Liability

Design responsibility coverage:

**Design-Build Projects:**
- $2,000,000 minimum coverage
- 3-year tail coverage
- Equipment selection liability
- System performance guarantees

**HVAC Design Liability:**
- Load calculation errors
- Equipment sizing deficiencies
- System performance shortfalls
- Energy code compliance
- Indoor air quality issues

### Installation Floater

Equipment in transit coverage:

- Chiller transportation
- Rigging operations
- Crane operations
- Rooftop equipment placement
- Specialized HVAC equipment

### Additional Insured

Owner protection requirements:

- Primary and non-contributory
- Blanket additional insured
- Products/completed operations
- Waiver of subrogation
- 30-day cancellation notice

### Certificate Management

Insurance documentation:

- ACORD 25 certificate form
- Policy endorsements attached
- Expiration date tracking
- Renewal requirements
- Continuous coverage verification

## Notice to Proceed

### Issuance Conditions

Requirements before NTP:

**Administrative Prerequisites:**
- Fully executed contract
- Performance bond received
- Payment bond received
- Insurance certificates approved
- Building permit obtained (if required)

**HVAC-Specific Prerequisites:**
- Equipment submittals scheduled
- Long-lead items identified
- Fabrication shop qualified
- Coordination meeting scheduled
- BIM/CAD files exchanged

### NTP Content

Formal authorization elements:

**Required Information:**
- Project name and number
- Contract commencement date
- Substantial completion deadline
- Final completion deadline
- Liquidated damages rate
- Mobilization requirements

**HVAC Schedule Milestones:**
- Equipment submittal deadlines
- Approval duration expectations
- Procurement timelines
- Delivery dates
- Installation sequences
- Testing and commissioning dates

### Effective Date

Contract time commencement:

- Specified calendar date
- Date of NTP issuance
- Date of permit issuance
- Site access date
- Owner determination

### Conditional NTP

Partial authorization scenarios:

**Limited Scope Authorization:**
- Submittal preparation only
- Shop drawing development
- Equipment procurement
- Material ordering
- Fabrication commencement

**HVAC Applications:**
- Long-lead chiller procurement
- Custom air handler fabrication
- Controls programming
- Ductwork shop drawings
- Factory witness testing

### Delayed NTP

Managing procurement during delays:

- Submittal approval continuation
- Equipment ordering authorization
- Material purchase limits
- Storage arrangements
- Payment terms adjustment

## Financial Arrangements

### Retainage

Withheld payment provisions:

**Standard Practices:**
- 10% initial retainage (typical)
- 5% reduced retainage (at 50% completion)
- Release upon substantial completion
- Final release after punchlist

**HVAC Retainage Issues:**
- Equipment payment timing
- Stored materials retainage
- Commissioning completion
- Seasonal testing holdback
- Warranty activation timing

### Payment Schedule

Application for payment procedures:

- Monthly progress payments
- Stored materials provisions
- Equipment delivery payments
- Substantial completion payment
- Final payment release

### Mobilization Payment

Initial project funding:

- 5-10% of contract value
- One-time payment
- Shop drawing preparation
- Project management setup
- Quality control systems

The bid award procedure establishes the legal and financial framework for HVAC construction projects. Proper execution of these procedures protects all parties, ensures adequate resources, and sets the foundation for successful project delivery. Attention to HVAC-specific requirements during the award phase prevents delays and disputes during construction.
