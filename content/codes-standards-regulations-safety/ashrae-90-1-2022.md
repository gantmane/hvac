## ASHRAE 90.1-2022 Overview

ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2022 represents a significant advancement in building energy efficiency requirements, introducing new compliance pathways and updated efficiency metrics. The standard achieves approximately 15% energy cost reduction compared to the 2019 edition.

## Major Changes from 2019

### New Mechanical System Performance Path (MSPR)

The 2022 edition introduces Section 6.6, the Mechanical System Performance Path, as an alternative to prescriptive compliance. This path uses the **Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR)** to evaluate complete HVAC system efficiency.

**TSPR Calculation:**

```
TSPR = Annual Cooling Output + Annual Heating Output
       ─────────────────────────────────────────────
       Annual Energy Input (electricity + fuel)
```

**Minimum TSPR Requirements by Climate Zone:**

| Climate Zone | Minimum TSPR | Baseline System |
|--------------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1A, 2A | 3.8 | Packaged rooftop, VAV |
| 3A, 4A | 3.5 | Packaged rooftop, VAV |
| 5A, 6A | 3.2 | Boiler + chiller, VAV |
| 7, 8 | 2.8 | Boiler + chiller, VAV |

### Heat Recovery Chiller Credit (COPHR)

New metric for evaluating heat recovery during chiller operation:

```
COPHR = Useful Heating Output / Compressor Energy Input
```

Systems with COPHR ≥3.0 receive prescriptive trade-off credits.

### Updated Equipment Efficiency Requirements

**Air-Cooled Chillers (≥150 tons):**

| Metric | 2019 Requirement | 2022 Requirement | Improvement |
|--------|------------------|------------------|-------------|
| Full Load EER | 9.7 | 10.1 | +4.1% |
| IPLV | 12.5 | 13.2 | +5.6% |

**Water-Cooled Chillers (300-600 tons, positive displacement):**

| Metric | 2019 Requirement | 2022 Requirement | Improvement |
|--------|------------------|------------------|-------------|
| Full Load kW/ton | 0.56 | 0.52 | +7.1% |
| IPLV | 0.38 | 0.35 | +7.9% |

**Boilers (≥1,000,000 Btu/h):**

| Type | 2019 Efficiency | 2022 Efficiency |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Gas-fired, hot water | 82% Et | 90% Et (condensing) |
| Gas-fired, steam | 80% Et | 82% Et |

### Energy Recovery Ventilation

**Minimum Enthalpy Recovery Ratio (ERR):**

| Exhaust Airflow | Climate Zones 3B, 3C, 4B, 4C, 5B | All Other Zones |
|-----------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| ≥1,500 cfm | 50% | 55% |
| ≥5,000 cfm | 55% | 60% |
| ≥10,000 cfm | 60% | 65% |

### VRF and DOAS Requirements

**Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) Systems:**

| Cooling Capacity | Minimum IEER |
|------------------|--------------|
| <65,000 Btu/h | 16.0 |
| 65,000-135,000 Btu/h | 15.5 |
| >135,000 Btu/h | 14.5 |

**Dedicated Outdoor Air Systems (DOAS):**
- Mandatory energy recovery for systems >1,000 cfm outdoor air
- Demand control ventilation required for spaces >500 sq ft with design occupancy >25 people

## Compliance Pathways

### Path Comparison

| Pathway | Description | Best For |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Prescriptive (6.5) | Meet all individual equipment requirements | Simple systems, standard buildings |
| MSPR (6.6) | Meet TSPR for complete mechanical system | Complex systems, integrated design |
| Performance (11) | Whole-building energy modeling | High-performance, innovative designs |

### MSPR Advantages

1. **System Integration Credit**: Accounts for heat recovery, economizers, and controls synergies
2. **Equipment Trade-offs**: Higher efficiency equipment can offset lower efficiency elsewhere
3. **Innovation Allowance**: Enables emerging technologies not covered by prescriptive requirements

## Prescriptive Changes Summary

### Fan System Power

**Maximum Fan System Power (W/cfm):**

| System Type | 2019 Limit | 2022 Limit |
|-------------|------------|------------|
| CV systems, no heating/cooling | 0.30 | 0.28 |
| CV systems with heating/cooling | 0.60 | 0.55 |
| VAV systems | Variable | Variable (-5%) |

### Piping Insulation

**Increased Pipe Insulation Requirements:**

| Fluid Temp Range | Pipe Size | 2022 Insulation (inches) |
|------------------|-----------|-------------------------|
| 40-60°F | <1" | 1.0 |
| 40-60°F | 1"-4" | 1.5 |
| 40-60°F | >4" | 2.0 |

### Economizer Requirements

**Integrated Economizer Requirements:**

| Climate Zone | Cooling Capacity Threshold |
|--------------|---------------------------|
| 1A, 1B | Not required |
| 2A, 2B, 3A | ≥54,000 Btu/h |
| All others | ≥33,000 Btu/h |

## Controls Requirements

### Demand Control Ventilation (DCV)

Required for spaces meeting ALL criteria:
- Design occupancy ≥25 people
- Floor area ≥500 sq ft
- Design outdoor airflow >0.3 cfm/sq ft

### Setpoint Reset

**Supply Air Temperature Reset:**
- Required for all VAV systems >10,000 cfm
- Minimum reset range: 5°F from design
- Based on zone demand or outdoor air temperature

**Chilled Water Reset:**
- Required for chilled water systems >300,000 Btu/h
- Reset based on valve position or outdoor air

## Documentation Requirements

### Compliance Documentation Package

1. **Mechanical schedules** with efficiency ratings
2. **TSPR calculations** (if using MSPR path)
3. **Controls sequences** demonstrating required strategies
4. **Commissioning plan** per Section 8

### Testing and Balancing

- Air system balancing within ±10% of design
- Hydronic balancing within ±5% of design
- Economizer functional testing required
